Savings & investments

Transfers to/from your savings account(s) netted into a savings balance. Large one-off movements (≥ the threshold in ⚙ settings) are flagged incidental and excluded from the regular net, which captures the monthly saving rhythm — including the small bookings back to current that quietly eat into it. Use the toggle on any row to reclassify. Investments are one-way yearly injections: broker accounts are auto-detected from the counterparty (DEGIRO, Meesman, Brand New Day, ASN Doelbeleggen, …) and can be added by IBAN in ⚙ settings.

Manage projects & budgets

Rename, re-budget, re-schedule, add or remove sub-project categories. These drive the triage dropdowns, the tracker above and the plan line of the projection — removing a project or changing its budget/year recalculates the MJP graph. Renaming a project re-labels every transaction already mapped to it. Changes persist in localStorage.

Upload new ASN exports (transactiehistorie_*.csv) at any time — duplicates are de-duplicated by date + sequence number + amount. Incidental spend is deducted in the year it actually happens and relieves the project's budget in its planned MJP year (e.g. the €2.000 Heattransformers payment consumes part of the Warmtepomp budget in 2026). Projects done earlier, later, cheaper or more expensive than planned all flow through correctly; only spend without budget left dents the projection.
